# New Season (GW1) & Preseason Blueprint

Use this guide when a manager is starting from GW1 or rebuilding before Gameweek 1 deadline.

## 1. Pre-GW1 Rule Verification
Before recommending GW1 structure, verify if the new season introduced any scoring, chip, FT, or price changes on `premierleague.com` and cache the summary in `~/.fpl-guru/season_rules.md`.

## 2. Optimal GW1 Squad Architecture
- **Balance Flexibility Over Perfection:** Avoid locking into dead-end £4.0m/£4.5m non-playing fodder just to force three max-priced premiums. Leave **£0.5m–£1.0m** liquidity or clean price-point stepping stones (`£5.5m`, `£6.5m`, `£7.5m`).
- **4-5-1 / 3-4-3 / 3-5-2 Flexibility:** Build a bench with at least **2 nailed starters** (high-DEFCON budget DEF/MID) to absorb early-season rotation and new-signing bedding-in periods.
- **Minutes Certainty First:** Prioritize 90-minute penalty/set-piece takers and proven Premier League starters over speculative summer signings until lineups stabilize in GW1–3.

## 3. GW1–GW4 Execution Plan
- **Bank Transfers Early:** Aim to roll GW1 -> GW2 -> GW3 to build **3+ Free Transfers** by the first international break / GW4 fixture swing.
- **Avoid Early Knee-Jerks:** 1–2 blanks from a high-xGI player with green fixtures are variance, not a sell signal.
- **First Wildcard Window:** Use GW4–GW8 only when 4+ structural fixes align with a clear fixture swing—otherwise leverage banked FTs (up to 5) to save WC1 for a later pivot.
